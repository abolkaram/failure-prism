# Product Boundary

## Intelligent Contract owns

- Plans, protected requirements, categories, attacks, patch failures, and terminal state.
- Reviewer and owner authorization.
- Validator-governed attack materiality and patch closure.
- Deterministic state transitions and paged reads.

## Frontend owns

- Radial navigation and field validation.
- Wallet connection and transaction submission.
- Transaction status presentation and read-only browsing.
- No authoritative cached state.
