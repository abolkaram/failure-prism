# Frontend Design Contract

## screenArchitecture
mechanical-control-panel

## aboveTheFold
A full-height circular containment chamber occupies the center. Threat sectors form its perimeter, the selected sector opens into a radial probe drawer, and the plan core shows its current pressure state.

## primaryInteraction
Select one unsealed threat sector, rotate it into the inspection gate, and submit either a material attack or the owner's patch without leaving the chamber.

## layoutMap
Thin top instrument rail, central radial prism, left plan aperture, right active-pressure readout, and a bottom transaction rail that expands only while needed.

## visualMetaphor
industrial optical containment chamber

## motionSystem
prism boot, sector orbit, aperture focus, pressure surge, transaction refraction, seal anneal

## effectStack
CSS conic chamber, animated SVG refraction paths, subtle procedural grid

## componentShapeLanguage
machined circles, clipped instrument plates, etched ticks, narrow status apertures

## customVisualComponents
ContainmentPrism, ThreatRotor, PressureAperture, SealRing, RefractionTransactionRail

## bannedFromThisBuild
No generic centered hero, equal feature cards, horizontal stats strip, chip row, submit-form-to-feed skeleton, three-column footer, navy-purple gradient, or left-center-right dashboard rail.

## proofOfDifference
Unlike Story Splice and Repair Radio, this is a radial single-surface pressure instrument. Inputs emerge from selected sectors, results anneal into the same ring, and no timeline, oscilloscope, stacked form, or feed is used.
